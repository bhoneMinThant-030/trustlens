# Fees and charges for OCBC credit cards

Version date: 1 October 2025.

## Late payment charge

- Credit cards: S$100 if the minimum payment is not received by the payment due date.
- Balance transfers: S$120 if the minimum payment is not received by the payment due date.

## Interest

- Purchases: 27.78% per annum. If full payment is not received by the due date, a minimum charge of S$2.50 a month applies.
- If the minimum payment is not received by the due date, the rate on the outstanding balance and new purchases rises to 30.78% per annum. It reverts to the prevailing rate once the account is not past due for the current and last two months.
- Cash advances: 28.92% per annum on the amount withdrawn, compounded if monthly interest is not paid in full (minimum charge S$2.50).

## Cash advance fee

S$15 or 8% of the amount withdrawn, whichever is greater.

## Overlimit fee

S$50 if the total outstanding balance across all your credit cards exceeds your total credit limit. Charged to the card with the earliest statement cycle and highest outstanding balance.

## Foreign currency transactions

- Transactions in currencies other than USD are converted to USD, then to SGD, at rates set by the card associations.
- From 3 December 2019: a 1% card association conversion charge plus a 2.25% bank administrative fee.
- Before 3 December 2019: a 1% card association conversion charge plus a 2% bank administrative fee.
- An additional 1% fee applies to Visa and Mastercard transactions charged in SGD but processed overseas, including dynamic currency conversion and overseas online purchases charged in SGD.

## Transaction activity fee

8% of the transaction amount or S$15, whichever is higher, on betting transactions under Merchant Category Code 7995.

## Retrieval fees (inclusive of GST)

- Sales draft: S$15 per copy, or S$25 for the original.
- Statements: free for the current to 2 months; S$6 each for 3 to 12 months; S$33 each for more than 12 months.

## Card replacement

- OCBC FRANK Credit Card: replacement fee waived for up to 2 replacements in a 12-month period. From the 3rd replacement onwards, S$32.70 applies.

## Lost or stolen card liability

Cardmember liability for a lost or stolen card: S$100, with reference to clause 9 of the ABS guide "What You Should Know About Credit Cards".
