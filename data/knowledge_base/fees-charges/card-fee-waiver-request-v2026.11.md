# How to request a credit card fee waiver

Customers can request a credit card fee waiver themselves through digital banking.

## OCBC app

1. Log in with your access code and PIN, or with OCBC OneLook or OCBC OneTouch.
2. Tap "More" at the bottom right.
3. Under "Card Services", tap "Request fee waiver".
4. Select the card and submit the request.

## OCBC Internet Banking (desktop)

1. Log in.
2. Click "Customer service".
3. Under "Cards", select "Credit card fee waiver".
4. Select the card and submit the request.

## Notes

The guide explains how to submit a fee-waiver request but does not specify eligible fees, approval criteria or guarantee approval.
