# Fees and charges for OCBC credit cards

Version date: 1 June 2023.

## Late payment charge

S$80 if the minimum payment is not received by the payment due date.

## Interest

- Purchases: 26.88% per annum.
- Cash advances: 28.00% per annum.

## Cash advance fee

S$15 or 6% of the amount withdrawn, whichever is greater.

## Overlimit fee

S$40 if the total outstanding balance exceeds the total credit limit.

## Foreign currency transactions

A 1% card association conversion charge plus a 2.25% bank administrative fee.

## Card replacement

OCBC FRANK Credit Card: S$25 per replacement card.
