# OCBC 365 Credit Card: features, fees and eligibility

## Cashback (from 1 November 2026)

- 6% cashback on dining (including fast food), groceries, land transport and petrol, in Singapore and overseas.
- 3% cashback on eligible Watsons purchases until 31 March 2027, subject to the S$800 monthly minimum spend; otherwise 0.25%. Marketplace purchases are excluded from the 3% rate. From 1 April 2027, 0.25% applies.
- Up to 1% cashback on eligible advertising platform spend (1% with full payment, 0.5% with instalments).
- 0.25% base cashback on other eligible transactions.

## Minimum spend and cap

- Minimum spend of S$800 per calendar month to earn the 6% rate. If it is not met, the 0.25% base rate applies.
- Monthly cashback is capped at S$160, with no separate category caps. Eligible advertising platform cashback is uncapped and requires no minimum spend.

## Annual fee

- Principal card: S$196.20 (including GST), waived for the first two years.
- Supplementary card: S$98.10 (including GST), waived for the first two years.
- The annual fee is automatically waived with a minimum spend of S$10,000 in one year, counted from the card issue month.

## Eligibility

- Minimum age: 21.
- Singaporeans and PRs aged 21 to 55: annual income of at least S$30,000.
- Singaporeans and PRs aged above 55: annual income of at least S$15,000.
- Foreigners aged 21 and above: annual income of at least S$45,000.

## Welcome promotion

New OCBC credit cardmembers can receive S$365 cashback and a 2-year annual fee waiver with a minimum eligible spend of S$800 within 60 days of card approval. Valid till 31 December 2026.
