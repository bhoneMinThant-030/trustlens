# OCBC personal credit card range overview

As listed on the OCBC credit cards page. Where the page itself showed different figures in different sections, both are recorded.

## Cashback cards

- OCBC INFINITY Cashback Card: unlimited 1.6% cashback on all eligible local and overseas spend.
- OCBC 365 Credit Card: 6% cashback on dining (including fast food), groceries, land transport and petrol. Summary states up to S$160 monthly cashback; the fee section of the same page states total cashback is capped at S$80 per month. Annual fee shown: S$192.60 a year (first 2 years free).
- OCBC FRANK Credit Card: up to 10% monthly cashback, capped at S$100, with a minimum spend of S$800 per month. Annual fee: S$80 a year (first 2 years free).
- OCBC Great Eastern Cashflo Credit Card: up to 1.2% rebate on other eligible transactions, capped at S$100 monthly. Annual fee: S$192.60 a year (first 2 years free).
- OCBC NXT Credit Card: 1% cash rebate with no minimum spend, capped at S$100 per month. Annual fee: S$192.60 a year (first 2 years free).
- BEST-OCBC Credit Card: 3% cash rebate at BEST Denki. Annual fee: S$160.50 a year (first 2 years free).

## Miles cards

- OCBC 90°N Card: 2.1 miles per S$1 foreign currency spend and 1.3 miles per S$1 local spend. Annual service fee shown: S$54.00 (waived for the first year).
- OCBC VOYAGE Card: 2.2 VOYAGE Miles per S$1 foreign currency spend and 1.3 VOYAGE Miles per S$1 local spend. Annual fee: S$492.50 (incl. GST), not waivable. Minimum income S$120,000 a year.
- OCBC Premier, Premier Private Client and Bank of Singapore VOYAGE Card (by invitation only): 2.3 VOYAGE Miles per S$1 foreign currency spend and 1.6 VOYAGE Miles per S$1 local spend.

## Rewards (OCBC$) cards

- OCBC Rewards Card: bonus 15X OCBC$ (6 miles) at Watsons, Lazada, Shopee, Taobao and TikTok Shop until 31 March 2027. Annual fee: S$192.60 a year (first 2 years free).
- OCBC Premier Visa Infinite Credit Card: 28 OCBC$ per S$5 foreign currency spend and 16 OCBC$ per S$5 local spend. Annual fee: free.
- OCBC Platinum Credit Card: OCBC$ on every dollar spent. Annual fee: S$160.50 a year (first 2 years free).
- OCBC Arts Credit Card: bonus OCBC$ on SISTIC purchases. Annual fee: S$160.50 a year (first 2 years free).
