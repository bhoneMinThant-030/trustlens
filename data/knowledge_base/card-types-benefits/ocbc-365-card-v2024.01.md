# OCBC 365 Credit Card: features, fees and eligibility

## Cashback

- 5% cashback on dining.
- 3% cashback on groceries, land transport, recurring telco and electricity bills, and streaming.
- 6% cashback on petrol.
- 0.25% base cashback on other eligible transactions.

## Minimum spend and cap

- Minimum spend of S$800 per calendar month to earn the bonus rates.
- Total cashback is capped at S$80 per month.

## Annual fee

- Principal card: S$194.40 (including GST), waived for the first two years.
- The annual fee is automatically waived with a minimum spend of S$2,000 in one year.

## Eligibility

- Minimum age: 21.
- Singaporeans and PRs: annual income of at least S$30,000.
- Foreigners: annual income of at least S$45,000.
