# OCBC PayLite instalment payment plan

## What it is

PayLite splits OCBC credit card retail purchases of at least S$100 each into interest-free monthly instalments.

## Tenures and fees

- Tenures: 3, 6, 12 or 24 months.
- One-time processing fee: from 1.00% to 3.00% of the converted amount, depending on tenure.

## How to apply

Apply through the OCBC app or OCBC Internet Banking.
