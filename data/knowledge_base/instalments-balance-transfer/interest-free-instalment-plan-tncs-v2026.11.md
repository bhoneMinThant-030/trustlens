# Interest-free Instalment Plan terms and conditions

## Number of instalments

The number of instalments is as indicated on the receipt containing the details of the Plan.

## Late instalments (clause e)

If an instalment is not paid in full when due, the cardmember must pay finance charges, interest and fees on the outstanding amounts at the Bank's prevailing rate.

## Credit limit (clause d)

Unpaid instalment amounts reduce the available credit limit.

## Cancellation or termination (clause g)

If the Plan or the card is cancelled or terminated before full settlement, an administrative fee of S$150 is payable in addition to full and final settlement of the outstanding amount. The Bank may revise this fee at its discretion.

## Prepayment (clause h)

The Bank may at its discretion impose an administrative fee on termination or prepayment.

## Default and termination by the Bank (clauses f and i)

The Bank may, without notice, debit the whole outstanding balance to the card account, which then becomes immediately due and payable. The Bank may terminate the Plan without prior notice.

## Digital payments

The Bank may not offer a Plan for amounts charged via a Digital Payment System.
