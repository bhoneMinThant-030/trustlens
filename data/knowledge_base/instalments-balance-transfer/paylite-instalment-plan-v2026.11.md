# OCBC PayLite instalment payment plan

## What it is

PayLite splits OCBC credit card retail purchases of at least S$50 each into interest-free monthly instalments. There is no maximum amount.

## Eligibility

- Principal cardholders of an OCBC credit card.
- Not available for the OCBC NXT Card, the OCBC Great Eastern Cashflo Card, or customers with a Debt Consolidation facility.
- Eligible: retail transactions from local, overseas or online merchants.
- Not eligible: cash advances, balance transfers, bank fees and charges, 0% IPP transactions, cash-on-instalments transactions, and other transactions the Bank may determine.

## Tenures and fees

- Tenures: 3, 6 or 12 months.
- One-time processing fee: 3% for 3 and 6 months; 5% for 12 months.
- Effective interest rate (EIR), including the processing fee: 18.18% p.a. (3 months), 10.43% p.a. (6 months), 9.50% p.a. (12 months).

## How to apply

- OCBC app: Cards > select the card > PayLite > choose transactions and tenure > Submit.
- OCBC Internet Banking.
- Phone: 1800 363 3333.
- If approved, instalment details appear in the card transaction history the next day.
