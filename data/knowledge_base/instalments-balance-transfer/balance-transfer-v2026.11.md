# OCBC Balance Transfer

## What it is

Balance Transfer lets customers take cash from the unused credit limit of an existing OCBC credit card or EasiCredit account, at 0% nominal interest for the chosen tenor. Approval is at the Bank's sole discretion.

## Interest and fees

- 0% interest during the approved tenor. Afterwards, outstanding credit-card Balance Transfers attract the prevailing cash-advance rate or another rate specified by OCBC. EasiCredit transfers revert to the prevailing EasiCredit rate.
- Illustrative processing fees: 1.80% for 3 months (EIR 7.38% p.a.), 2.50% for 6 months (EIR 5.34% p.a.), and 4.50% for 12 months (EIR 5.20% p.a.). Actual offers depend on the applicant’s credit profile and bank approval.
- The processing fee is charged to the card or EasiCredit account.
- Credit-card Balance Transfer late payment charge: S$120 if the minimum payment is not received by the due date. EasiCredit charges follow its applicable terms.

## Amount and repayment

- Minimum amount: S$500.
- Minimum monthly repayment: follow the billing statement. The website illustrates subsequent monthly payments of 3% of the outstanding balance or S$50, whichever is higher. Paying only the minimum may leave an outstanding balance at the end of the tenor, which will attract the applicable post-tenor interest.

## Eligibility

- Minimum age 21.
- Principal cardholder of an OCBC credit card, or an OCBC EasiCredit account holder.
- The underlying credit-card or EasiCredit account must be in good standing.

## How to apply

OCBC app: More > Apply > Loans > Balance Transfer > Get started, then enter the amount, choose accounts and submit.
