# OCBC Balance Transfer

## What it is

Balance Transfer lets customers take cash from the unused credit limit of an existing OCBC credit card or EasiCredit account, at 0% nominal interest for the chosen tenor. Approval is at the Bank's sole discretion.

## Interest and fees

- 0% interest for the tenor. After the repayment period, the rate reverts to the prevailing rate for the card or EasiCredit account.
- One-time processing fee: 1.80% for 3 months (EIR 7.38% p.a.), 2.50% for 6 months (EIR 5.34% p.a.), 4.50% for 12 months (EIR 5.20% p.a.).
- The processing fee is charged to the card or EasiCredit account.
- Late payment charge on balance transfers: S$120 if the minimum payment is not received by the due date (from the Fees and Charges for Credit Cards document).

## Amount and repayment

- Minimum amount: S$500.
- Monthly repayment: 3% of the outstanding balance or S$50, whichever is higher.

## Eligibility

- Minimum age 21.
- Principal cardholder of an OCBC credit card, or an OCBC EasiCredit account holder.

## How to apply

OCBC app: More > Apply > Loans > Balance Transfer > Get started, then enter the amount, choose accounts and submit.
