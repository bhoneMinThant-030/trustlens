# Changes to bill payments using OCBC credit cards

## What changed

From 10 October 2026, customers can no longer use an OCBC credit card to pay 17 listed billing organisations through the OCBC app or Internet Banking. The list includes Courts, National Service Resort & Country Club, Great Eastern General Insurance, M1, NUS Society, SAFRA, Orchid Country Club and ten Town Councils.

## How to pay these organisations now

- Customers can continue to pay these billing organisations using an OCBC Current or Savings Account.
- In the OCBC app: Pay & Transfers > Bills > Billing Orgs, select the organisation, enter the amount and choose the account.
- Customers without an OCBC Current or Savings Account can open one, or use the payment options each organisation provides.

## Effect on the credit card

The change does not affect the credit card itself. It can still be used for other eligible transactions.
