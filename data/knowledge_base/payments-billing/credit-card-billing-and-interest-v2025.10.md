# Credit card billing: interest-free period, minimum payment and interest

From the OCBC Fees and Charges for Credit Cards document, version date 1 October 2025.

## Interest-free period

Up to 23 calendar days from the statement date, provided there is no balance carried forward from the previous statement.

## Minimum monthly payment

- Within the credit limit: S$50 or 3% of the total balance, whichever is higher, plus any overdue amount.
- Over the credit limit: 3% of the total balance, plus the amount in excess of the credit limit, plus any overdue amount.

## Late payment

S$100 late payment charge if the minimum payment is not received by the payment due date.

## Interest on unpaid balances

- Purchases: 27.78% per annum if full payment is not received by the due date. Interest is calculated from the transaction date, with a minimum charge of S$2.50 a month.
- If the minimum payment is not received by the due date, the rate on the outstanding balance and new purchases increases to 30.78% per annum, calculated from each purchase's transaction date. It reverts to the prevailing rate when the account is no longer past due in the current and last two months.

## Payment hierarchy

If full payment is not received by the due date, payments are applied first to the balance with the highest interest rate.
