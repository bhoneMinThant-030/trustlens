# Staff quick reference: OCBC 365 cashback

Informal notes circulated among branch staff.

## Cashback cap

The OCBC 365 card has a monthly cashback cap of S$250, with no category limits.

## Minimum spend

There is no minimum monthly spend for the dining cashback.

## Crediting

Cashback is credited within the same month.
