# Terms and conditions governing OCBC 365 Credit Card Cashback Programme

Version date: 1 March 2024.

## Cashback rates (Clause 2)

- 2A(a): 5% cashback on "Dining" card transactions.
- 2A(b): 3% cashback on "Groceries".
- 2A(c): 3% cashback on "Land Transport".
- 2A(d): 6% cashback on "Petrol".
- 2A(e): 3% cashback on recurring telecommunications and electricity bills.
- 2A(f): 3% cashback on "Watsons".
- 2A(g): 3% cashback on "Streaming".
- 2A(h): 3% cashback on "Electric Vehicle Charging".
- 2B: 0.25% base cashback.

## Minimum spend and cap (Clause 3)

- The Minimum Spend Requirement must be met to receive the Clause 2A cashback. Base cashback has no minimum.
- Spend can be aggregated across the Principal and Supplementary Cardmembers.
- Minimum spend of S$800 in a calendar month: cashback capped at S$80.
- Minimum spend of S$1,600 in a calendar month: cashback capped at S$160.

## Excluded transactions

- Excluded from the Minimum Spend Requirement: MCCs including tolls (4784), wire transfers (4829), quasi-cash including cryptocurrency (6051), insurance (6300, 5960), stored value card loads (6540), gambling (7995), hospitals (8062), education (8211 to 8299), charities and religious organisations (8398, 8661), and government services, fines and tax payments (9211 to 9405).
- Not eligible for cashback: annual card fees, Cash-On-Instalments, Instalment Payment Plan, PayLite, tax payments, interest, late payment charges, cash advances, balance transfers, bill payments via Internet Banking, AXS or SAM, EZ-Link and Transit Link top-ups, Shopee Pay, and other fees and charges.

## Crediting (Clause 4)

Cashback is credited to the Principal Cardmember's card account in the following month and offset against that month's billed amount.
