# Terms and conditions governing OCBC 365 Credit Card Cashback Programme

With effect from 1 November 2026.

## Cashback rates (Clause 2)

- 2A(a): 6% cashback on "Dining", "Groceries", "Land Transport" and "Petrol", subject to the Minimum Spend Requirement.
- 2A(b): 3% cashback on "Watsons" (valid till 31 March 2027), subject to the Minimum Spend Requirement.
- 2B: 0.25% cashback on all card transactions if the Minimum Spend Requirement is not met, and on transactions outside Clause 2A if it is met.
- 2C: 1% cashback on "Advertising Platform" with full payment.
- 2D: 0.5% cashback on "Advertising Platform" with payment in instalments.

## Eligible categories

- Dining: MCC 5811 (Caterers), 5812 (Eating Places and Restaurants), 5814 (Fast Food Restaurants). Excludes hotel dining and wedding banquets not under these MCCs, and telephone or mail order transactions.
- Groceries: MCC 5411 (Grocery Stores and Supermarkets).
- Land Transport: MCC 4011, 4111, 4112, 4121, 4131. Excludes EZ-Link and Transit Link top-ups.
- Petrol: MCC 5541 and 5542. Excludes fleet card top-ups.
- Watsons: transactions under merchant description "WATSONS*" and "Watsons", excluding purchases on online marketplaces such as Lazada, Shopee and Amazon.

## Minimum spend and cap (Clause 3)

- The Minimum Spend Requirement of S$800 in a calendar month must be met to receive the Clause 2A cashback.
- Spend can be aggregated between the Principal and Supplementary Cardmembers.
- The maximum Clause 2A cashback one account (Principal and all Supplementary Cardmembers together) can earn in a calendar month is S$160.
- Clauses 2C and 2D have no minimum spend and no cap.

## Excluded transactions

Transactions on the OCBC Cards Rewards Transaction Exclusion List, as amended from time to time, do not count towards the Minimum Spend Requirement and do not earn cashback.

## Crediting (Clause 4)

- Cashback is credited to the Principal Cardmember's card account in the following month, based on posted transactions.
- Cashback earned by Supplementary Cardmembers is credited to the Principal Cardmember's account.
- Cashback is offset against that month's billed amount.
