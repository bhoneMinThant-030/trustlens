# Credit card minimum income requirements

## Standard income requirements

Most OCBC personal credit cards share the same minimum annual income requirements:

- Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents aged 21 to 55: at least S$30,000 a year
- Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents aged above 55: at least S$15,000 a year
- Foreigners aged 21 and above: at least S$45,000 a year

The minimum age for a principal cardmember is 21.

## Exceptions

- OCBC VOYAGE Card: minimum annual income of S$120,000.
- OCBC Premier, Premier Private Client and Bank of Singapore VOYAGE Cards are by invitation only.

## Approval

Meeting the minimum income does not guarantee approval. All applications are subject to the Bank's approval.
