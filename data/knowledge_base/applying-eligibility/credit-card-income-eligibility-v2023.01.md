# Credit card minimum income requirements

## Standard income requirements

- Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents aged 21 and above: at least S$30,000 a year.
- Foreigners aged 21 and above: at least S$60,000 a year.

The minimum age for a principal cardmember is 21.

## Exceptions

- OCBC VOYAGE Card: minimum annual income of S$150,000.

## Approval

All applications are subject to the Bank's approval.
