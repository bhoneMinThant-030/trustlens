# Credit card applications: eligibility and income documents

## Minimum age

To apply for a principal credit card, the applicant must be at least 21 years old. This applies to both Singaporeans/Permanent Residents and foreigners.

## Approval

All applications are subject to the Bank's approval.

## Income documents: Singaporeans and Permanent Residents (salaried employees)

Accepted income documents for new OCBC credit cardmembers who are salaried employees:

- (a) Computerised payslip, submitted together with one of (b), (c) or (d)
- (b) Salary crediting bank statement
- (c) CPF Contribution Statement and (d) Income Tax Notice of Assessment, which must be submitted together
- (e) Company letter certifying employment and salary, submitted with one of (a) to (c)

## Income documents: other applicant types

The page lists additional accepted documents for applicants with variable or commission-based income, self-employed applicants, applicants aged above 55, and applicants relying on rental or dividend income. These include CPF statements, CPF LIFE or Retirement Sum Scheme payout records, a tenancy agreement, and SGX/CDP statements.

## Income documents: foreigners (salaried employees)

- (a) Computerised payslip, submitted together with one of (b) or (c)
- (b) Salary crediting bank statement
- (c) Income Tax Notice of Assessment
- (d) Company letter certifying employment and salary, submitted with one of (a) to (c)
