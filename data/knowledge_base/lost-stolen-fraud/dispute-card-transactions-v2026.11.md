# Disputing card transactions

## How to dispute

Customers can dispute card transactions through OCBC Internet Banking or the OCBC app. For fraud-related disputes involving unauthorised transactions, up to 35 Visa transactions and 15 Mastercard transactions can be disputed in one submission.

## Types of disputes

- Unauthorised transactions.
- Merchant non-delivery, defective goods, or unsatisfactory products or services.
- Billing errors.

## Time limit for unauthorised transactions

Report an unauthorised transaction within 7 days of receiving the statement, or immediately after receiving an SMS alert about the transaction.

## Liability and authenticated transactions

- Cardmembers may not be liable for unauthorised card transactions, provided the transactions were not authorised via OTP or OCBC OneToken, and the card was not lost or stolen and used in person.
- Under the fraud-related dispute rules for unauthorised transactions, there are no dispute rights for fully authenticated transactions, such as those authorised via OTP.

## Supporting documents and decisions

Customers must provide the information or supporting documents the Bank requests so the transaction can be reported to Visa or Mastercard. All decisions are at the sole discretion of the card association.
