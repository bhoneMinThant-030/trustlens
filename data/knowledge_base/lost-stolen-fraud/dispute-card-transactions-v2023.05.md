# Disputing card transactions

## How to dispute

Customers can dispute card transactions through OCBC Internet Banking.

## Time limit for unauthorised transactions

Report an unauthorised transaction within 30 days of the statement date.

## Supporting documents

Customers must provide the documents the Bank requests.
