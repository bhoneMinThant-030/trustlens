# Impersonation scams: what OCBC will never do

## What OCBC will never do

- OCBC employees will never ask you to reveal your PIN/OTP or transfer funds to personal accounts.
- OCBC will never send you SMSes or emails with clickable URLs.
- Banks will never transfer calls to external parties like the Police or government officials.
- Never give out your Online Banking login credentials (Access Code and PIN) to anyone, including people claiming to be from OCBC.

## Warning signs

- Unsolicited contact by phone, email, SMS or messaging app.
- Claims of an overdue bill, court summons, criminal case, or frozen or compromised account.
- Pressure to act quickly, threats, or a transfer to the police or a government official.
- Requests for logins, card details, OTPs, or Singpass verification via QR code.
- Unexpected calls with a "+65" prefix or from overseas.

## If you suspect a scam

1. Hang up and do not call numbers or click links from suspicious messages.
2. Verify independently through the official website, app or hotline.
3. Call OCBC on 1800 363 3333 (or +65 6363 3333 from overseas) and press 8 to report fraud or temporarily freeze your accounts.
4. Use the kill switch in the OCBC app or at selected OCBC ATMs.
5. Report to the police.
6. Check with ScamShield by calling 1799 or visiting scamshield.gov.sg.
