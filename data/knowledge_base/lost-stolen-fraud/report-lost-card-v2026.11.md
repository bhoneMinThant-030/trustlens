# Reporting a lost or stolen card

## How to report

- Call OCBC on +65 6363 3333, or report the loss via the OCBC app.
- The service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

## What happens after reporting

- All functions of the card are suspended.
- All future transactions made with the card are declined immediately.

## Liability

The cardmember is not liable for any unauthorised transactions carried out after notifying the Bank of the loss.
